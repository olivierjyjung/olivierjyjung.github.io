about me ♡

I study how people talk to each other: what they say, and how they say it.

I combine language analysis with prosody and timing to build computational models that stay interpretable. That work has taken me from depth and responsiveness in facilitated group conversations to early signs of cognitive and affective decline in how a person talks.

I also run behavioral experiments on what happens to trust and memory when a voice turns out to be synthetic.

Speech & Language Processing, Human–AI Interaction, Computational Communication
