About

Who I Am

I examine how AI systems reshape human communication, media trust, and public discourse.

Olivier Jiyoun Jung is a graduate researcher in Communication & Media at Ewha Womans University.
Working at the intersection of Human–AI Interaction and computational social science, she combines speech modeling, natural language processing, and computational methods to study AI-mediated trust, perception, and dialogue.

She combines methods across speech, vision, and text to investigate authenticity perception, AI-mediated dialogue, and the societal impact of synthetic media.
